Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

What we store

For each game: what the group is avoiding, the house rules, the dates and the time zone. For each player: the nickname they typed, when they joined, and, if they are out, the day, the “where” text they wrote, who they say got them, and emoji reactions.

What we don’t

No accounts, no email, no phone numbers, no contacts, no ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics scripts. Your browser keeps a random key so it remembers which player you are; it is stored on our side only as a one-way hash.

To stop abuse we count requests per connection for one hour, keyed by a one-way hash of the IP address. The IP address itself is not stored.

Who can see it

Anyone with a game’s link can see that game’s board. Only share the link with people you would show the board to.

How long

Games and everything in them are deleted automatically 60 days after the game’s last day. The person who started a game can delete it at any time; players can leave and their entry is removed.

Where

Dodgemas runs on Cloudflare (Pages and Durable Objects).

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/song-dodge.

About: Dodgemas is an independent scoreboard for song-dodging games.
