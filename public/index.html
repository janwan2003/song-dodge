Questions

What is the game?

Every December, millions of people try to avoid hearing one famous Christmas song from 1 December until the end of Christmas Eve. Hear the original recording and you’re out. Dodgemas keeps the score for your group: who is still in, who fell, where, and who sabotaged whom.

Do covers count?

Your group decides. The default house rule is “original recording only; covers and remixes don’t count”. The person who starts the game can change it.

Can I join late?

Yes. Anyone with the link can join until the last day. The board shows the day they joined.

Can I play with several groups?

Yes. Each game has its own link: one for the office, one for family, one for friends.

It asks for my name again. How do I get back in?

Chat apps open links in their own browser, which remembers you separately from your normal one. Ask the person who started the game to send you a personal link (“Players and personal links” on the board). To switch phones yourself, use “Open on another device” on the old one.

Does it work for other things?

Any “avoid it” game: dodge spoilers before a finale, a banned word at work, “first to mention work on holiday”. Pick your own dates.

Is it free? What do you store?

Free, no account, no ads. We store the game, the nicknames and what players write, and delete it all 60 days after the game ends. Privacy
