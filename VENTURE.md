# song-dodge

## Thesis

**Who:** Office teams, friend group chats, families and uni groups in the UK, Ireland, Nordics,
Netherlands, Germany, Australia/NZ and increasingly the US, who play the December game of avoiding
one famous 1984 Christmas song from 1 December to the end of Christmas Eve. Hearing the original
recording anywhere means you are out.

**What:** A no-login "last one standing" board for a group playing a song-avoidance game. One person
starts a game (the classic December song, or any song or word the group chooses: "the one
Christmas song with the whistle intro", "anyone who says 'Christmas' first") and sends the link.
Members join with a nickname. When someone is hit, they tap *I'm out* and add where it got them
(supermarket, radio, a colleague's sabotage) and, optionally, *who got me*. The board shows
survivors, the fallen with their one-line "cause of death" in a timeline, the day count, and a
saboteur table ("Jonas has taken out 3"). On 24 December at 23:59 the survivors are crowned in a
"Hall of the Survivors" card.

**Needs others:** yes. Last-one-standing only exists if others are playing; sabotage needs friends.

**Viral moment:** being knocked out. People already post "#…geddon, I'm out" on social media when hit
(the game's own rule); the "cause of death" card ("Day 3 · Out at Tesco self-checkout · Survivors 6/9")
is that post made shareable. The final survivors card on 24 December is the second moment.

**How often:** this is the weak point. The main game is once a year (24 days, daily drama inside it).
The board can run any "avoid it" game afterwards (spoiler dodge before a finale, a banned-word week
at work, "first to mention work on holiday"), but that is unproven. Priority 3 for this reason.

**Why now:** the game starts 2026-12-01 (10 days from today) and has national press coverage every
year (NPR, KQED, Scripps local TV stations, RNZ, LADbible, BBC Radio 1 references). We found no
tracker for it: people keep score in group chats and on social media.

### The six questions

1. **Why the group IS the input:** the board is made only of members surviving and falling; each
   elimination and each sabotage credit is another member's input. Solo, there is no "last one".
2. **Reveal moment:** each elimination (pushed into the link preview: "Day 6 · 5 of 9 still in"),
   and the 24 December 23:59 final reveal of the survivors.
3. **Ritual or deadline:** a fixed season (1–24 December) with a hard deadline; daily drama in
   between. For other games the creator sets start and end.
4. **30-second join:** open link → nickname → "I'm still in". One tap.
5. **Screenshot-worthy result:** the "I'm out" card (day, place, survivors left, saboteur if any)
   and the survivors card on Christmas Eve.
6. **Why a joiner creates their own:** most people play with several groups (office, family,
   friends) and one game shows the format instantly; the "I'm out" card links to "Start a game for
   your other group" (allowed even after being knocked out, since each group's game is separate).

## Our edge

- **Loop: needs others.** A last-one-standing board for one person is empty.
- **K estimate ≈ 1.4** = 8 invited per game (an office team or friend chat) × 60% who join (one tap,
  and the game is already known) × 30% of joiners who start a game for another group. Reason: people
  play in several circles and the format is learned in one glance. Weakest factor: the season is
  24 days; after Christmas the board must prove other "avoid it" games or the loop stops.
- Nothing else is built for it; the card turns an existing habit (posting that you are out) into a
  post that carries a link.
- **Risks:** "Whamageddon" is the creators' name and the band name is a trademark: never use either
  in the product name, domain or listing; describe the song neutrally and let groups type the song.
  The build window is very short (live by 2026-12-01 to catch day one; by ~12-05 still catches most
  of the season). Yearly frequency caps long-term value.

What exists and works:
- Create: the "1–24 December" preset (rolls to next year after 24 Dec), or custom dates (1–62 days, any "avoid it"
  game); what to avoid; house rules (default: original recording only, covers don't count, sabotage allowed); creator's
  time zone; no account.
- Join from the link with a nickname (duplicates refused). Late joiners are welcome; the board shows "from day N".
  Joining is refused after the end.
- "I heard it… I'm out": quick place chips (Supermarket, Radio, At work, …) plus free text, optional "Who got you?"
  (credits a saboteur). Not possible before the start or after the end. Undo within 15 minutes.
- Board: a red hero card with "N of M still in · day X of Y" and a progress bar; the still-in chips; the fallen
  timeline with day, place and saboteur, plus 4 emoji reactions; a saboteur table; after the end, "The survivors" and
  "You survived".
- Share cards, 1080x1350 PNG drawn in the browser: "I'M OUT" (day, place, who got you, survivors left), "STILL IN",
  "I SURVIVED", "THE SURVIVORS". Each card ends in a "start a game for your group" line with the URL. Sharing uses the
  share sheet with the file, and falls back to sharing the link plus save / press-and-hold.
- Joiner→creator loop: "Start a game for another group" sits on the out box, the end state and the footer; home lists
  "Your games".
- Live link preview: "Avoid <title> · Dodgemas" + "Starts Tue 1 Dec · 4 players so far" / "Day 6 of 24 · 5 of 9 still
  in. Last out: Sam (Supermarket)." / "Final · 3 of 9 survived".
- Host: edit the title and rules, delete the game, and a "Players and personal links" sheet: remove a player, or issue a
  fresh personal link for a player who lost access (typically joined inside WhatsApp/Instagram's in-app browser and
  later opened the link in their normal browser; the old token stops working). A duplicate-name join now says "Is that
  you on another phone or app? Ask <host> for your personal link". Players: a private link for another device, leave.
- Home: a season badge ("The season starts in N days, on 1 December" / "Day N of 24 · it's not too late"), an FAQ entry
  for "it asks for my name again". Unknown paths return a real 404 page (they returned the home page with 200 before).
- The board's 60 s refresh skips while someone is typing (it used to wipe a half-typed nickname on returning to the
  tab). Sheets close on Escape, take focus, lock page scroll, and free the card image on close.
- Rate limits per hashed IP: create 20/h, join 60/h, writes 600/h, reads 20000/h (high because whole offices share
  one IP). The board polls every 60 s while visible. Games auto-delete 60 days after the last day. Privacy page, OG
  image, icons, manifest. Search Console verified and sitemap submitted (`scripts/gsc.py`).
